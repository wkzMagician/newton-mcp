newton.solvers.SolverFluidAPIC#

class newton.solvers.SolverFluidAPIC(model, grid_resolution=(32, 32, 32), domain_size=(1.0, 1.0, 1.0), flip_ratio=0.95, pressure_iters=40, domain_min=(0.0, 0.0, 0.0), max_particles=250_000, density=1000.0, viscosity=0.001, surface_tension=0.072, coupling_iterations=1, coupling_relaxation=1.0, cfl_number=0.5, cloth_drag=1.0, cloth_permeability=0.0, boundary_friction=0.0)[source]#

Bases: SolverBase

Fixed-capacity APIC/FLIP liquid solver for scene pipelines.

Particles transfer mass, momentum, and their affine velocity matrix to a regular pressure grid. A red-black pressure solve projects the grid before PIC/FLIP transfer back to particles. The implementation intentionally owns its particle pool instead of treating Newton contact particles as liquid.

Parameters:

  • model – Newton model containing liquid particles and rigid colliders.

  • grid_resolution (tuple[int, int, int]) – Pressure-grid cell counts.

  • domain_size (tuple[float, float, float]) – Physical pressure-grid size [m].

  • flip_ratio (float) – FLIP contribution in the APIC/FLIP blend.

  • pressure_iters (int) – Pressure projection iteration count.

  • domain_min (tuple[float, float, float]) – Pressure-domain minimum [m].

  • max_particles (int) – Fixed particle-pool capacity.

  • density (float) – Liquid mass density [kg/m^3].

  • viscosity (float) – Kinematic viscosity [m^2/s].

  • surface_tension (float) – Surface tension [N/m].
